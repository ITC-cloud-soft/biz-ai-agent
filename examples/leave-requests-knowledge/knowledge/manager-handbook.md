Guidance for managers on deciding leave requests at Example Co. (a fictional company).

## Unanswered requests {#unanswered}

Decide every leave request within 2 working days. A request that waits longer is reported as unanswered to the requester and the managers, and the AI assistant reminds you once per request.

## Team coverage {#coverage}

At least half of a team must be at work on every working day. Before approving, check the team calendar for overlapping requests.

## Overlapping requests {#overlaps}

When requests overlap and coverage would fall below half, approve them in the order they were made. For the year-end period, rotate who gets priority from one year to the next.
