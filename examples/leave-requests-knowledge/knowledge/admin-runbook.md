Procedures only administrators of Example Co. (a fictional company) carry out.

## Deactivating an account {#deactivate}

Deactivate an employee's account on their last working day, in the employee administration screen. Deactivation ends the employee's sessions and every AI delegation at once.

## Quarterly audit export {#audit-export}

In the first week of every quarter, export the audit log of the previous quarter and file it in the compliance archive. The export contains IDs and actions only.
