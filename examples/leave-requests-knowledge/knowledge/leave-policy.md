The leave policy of Example Co. (a fictional company). It applies to every employee.

## Annual leave {#annual-leave}

Full-time employees get 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees get annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. New employees can take annual leave from their first day.

## Carry-over {#carry-over}

Up to 5 unused days of annual leave carry over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March; days not used by then are lost. Days beyond the 5 are not paid out.

## How to request leave {#request}

Request leave in Leave Requests at least 5 working days before the first day. A manager approves or rejects the request, on the web or with the approval card in chat. You can see the status of your own requests in Leave Requests at any time.

## Sick leave {#sick-leave}

Tell your manager on the morning of the first day. Sick leave of more than 3 working days in a row needs a doctor's certificate. Sick leave does not reduce annual leave.

## Approval rules for managers {#approval-rules roles=admin,manager}

A manager may approve up to 10 consecutive working days. Longer requests also need an administrator's approval. During the year-end blackout (15 to 31 December) at most 2 people of one team may be on leave at the same time.
