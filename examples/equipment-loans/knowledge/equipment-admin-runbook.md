Procedures only administrators of Example Co. (a fictional company) carry out for the shared equipment.

## Adding an item to the catalog {#catalog}

Add a new item in the equipment administration screen with its asset tag and kind. Mark it high value when its purchase price is 300,000 yen or more; every loan of a high-value item then needs a manager's approval.

## Quarterly stock check {#stock-check}

In the first week of every quarter, compare the items at the equipment desk with the catalog. List missing items by asset tag only and report them to the finance team.

## Retiring old equipment {#retire}

Retire an item that cannot be repaired or is older than 5 years. Wipe its storage, remove it from the catalog, and keep the retirement record with the asset tag and the date.
