The rules for borrowing shared equipment at Example Co. (a fictional company). They apply to every employee.

## Company equipment you can borrow {#what-to-borrow}

The shared pool has laptops, projectors and cameras. The catalog in Equipment Loans lists every item with its asset tag and shows whether it is on loan today. Shared equipment is for work only; do not lend it on to anyone else.

## How to request a loan {#request}

Request a loan in Equipment Loans: choose the item, the first day and the due date. You can book up to 90 days ahead. A single loan lasts at most 30 days. An item can only be booked once for the same days.

## When a loan needs approval {#approval}

A loan needs a manager's approval when the item is marked high value in the catalog, or when the loan is longer than 7 days (counted from the first day to the due date, both included). All cameras and the workstation laptops are high value. Nobody approves their own loan: when a manager borrows, another manager or an administrator decides. Every other loan is booked at once, without approval.

## Picking up equipment {#pickup}

Collect the item at the equipment desk on the first day of the loan. Check that the asset tag matches your loan and that the accessories are in the bag.

## Returning equipment {#returning}

Bring the item back to the equipment desk by the end of the due date, with every accessory (charger, cables, bag). Delete your files from laptops and memory cards before you return them. A loan ends when the desk records the return in Equipment Loans.

## Late returns {#overdue}

If an item is not back by its due date, Equipment Loans sends one reminder to you and to the managers. Return it as soon as you can, or ask a manager to agree a new due date. Repeated late returns can lead to a pause in borrowing.

## Damage and loss {#damage}

Report damage or loss to the equipment desk on the same day. Do not try to repair an item yourself. You do not pay for normal wear.

## Extending a loan {#extend}

A loan cannot be extended after it starts: request a new loan for the extra days. While a loan still waits for approval, you can change its dates, or ask the AI assistant to propose new dates for a manager to accept.

## Approval guidance for managers {#approval-guidance roles=admin,manager}

Decide a loan request within 1 working day. Approve a high-value item only for a business purpose named in the request. One employee may hold at most one high-value item at a time. During the event season (October and November) approve camera loans of at most 14 days.

## Handling overdue loans {#overdue-handling roles=admin,manager}

When a loan is 3 days overdue, talk to the borrower directly. After 7 days overdue, tell an administrator, who may pause the borrower's loans for 30 days. Write only loan IDs and dates in notes, never reasons or names.
