How the administrators of Example Co. (a fictional company) audit the approved expense claims.

## The monthly audit sample {#sample}

In the first week of every month, draw a random sample of 10 % of the claims approved in the previous month, at least 5 claims. Add every claim between 28,000 and 30,000 yen (near the approval limit), every claim submitted after the 30-day deadline and every claim that looks like a duplicate. The AI assistant may propose claims for the sample; an administrator accepts or rejects each proposal.

## Checking a sampled claim {#check}

For each sampled claim, ask the claimant for the paper receipt and compare it with the claim. Check that the approver was not the claimant, that the caps for lodging and meals were kept, and that one expense was not split into several claims (the same claimant, the same day, the same shop).

## Recording findings {#findings}

Record each finding in the audit log by claim ID and finding type only, never with names. An amount paid too much is repaid by the claimant through the next salary. When the same finding repeats for one employee, report it to the head of administration.

## Retention of records {#retention}

Keep the receipts of sampled claims and the audit log for 7 years. Delete the photos of receipts of claims that were not sampled at the end of the fiscal year.
