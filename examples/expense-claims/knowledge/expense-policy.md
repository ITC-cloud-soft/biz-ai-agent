The expense policy of Example Co. (a fictional company). It applies to every employee who pays a business expense with their own money and claims it back.

## What you can claim {#eligible}

You can claim expenses you paid for the company's business. Transport: trains and buses to clients, sites and business trips; a taxi only when there is no train or bus, after 22:00, or when you carry heavy equipment. Lodging on a business trip: up to 12,000 yen a night (15,000 yen in Tokyo). Meals with customers or partners (meeting expenses): up to 5,000 yen per person. Supplies for work when the office stock has none. Communication: mobile data or Wi-Fi bought for work while travelling.

## What you cannot claim {#not-claimable}

Commuting between home and your usual office is not claimable: the commuting allowance in your salary covers it. Also not claimable: your own meals when you are not travelling, alcohol at internal events, traffic or parking fines, gifts to colleagues, first-class or Green Car upgrades, and personal purchases.

## Receipts {#receipts}

Every claim needs a receipt that shows the date, the shop and the amount. A clear photo of the receipt is enough to submit; keep the paper original until the end of the fiscal year, because the audit may ask for it. For a train or bus fare under 1,000 yen paid with an IC card, the card's usage history replaces the receipt. A lost receipt can be replaced by a lost-receipt statement for up to 3,000 yen; a larger expense without a receipt cannot be claimed.

## Deadlines {#deadlines}

Submit a claim within 30 days of the day you paid. Expenses of March, the last month of the fiscal year, must be claimed by 5 April. A claim approved by the 20th of a month is paid with the salary of the following month.

## How to submit a claim {#submit}

Submit one claim per receipt in Expense Claims: the date you paid, the category (transport, lodging, meals, supplies, communication, other), the amount in yen and a short note with the business purpose. Write the purpose, not the names of the people you met. You can see the status of your own claims in Expense Claims at any time.

## Who approves a claim {#who-approves}

A claim of up to 30,000 yen is approved by your own manager; a claim over 30,000 yen by an administrator. Nobody approves their own claim. When a claim waits more than 3 days for a decision, the approver is reminded once.

## Corrections proposed by the AI assistant {#corrections}

The AI assistant may propose a correction of a pending claim: the date, the category or the note. You accept or reject the correction on the claim's screen; nothing changes until you accept. The AI never approves a claim and never changes an amount: to change an amount, ask your approver to reject the claim and submit a new one.

## Approval limits for managers {#approval-limits roles=admin,manager}

A manager decides the claims of their own team of up to 30,000 yen. A claim over 30,000 yen goes to an administrator automatically; a manager cannot approve it. One expense must not be split into several claims to stay under the limit: reject split claims and tell the claimant to submit one claim. A manager never decides their own claim; it goes to their own manager.

## How to check a claim {#checking roles=admin,manager}

Before you approve, check that the receipt matches the date and the amount, that the category fits the expense, that the note states the business purpose, that the claim was submitted within 30 days, and that the same receipt was not claimed twice. Check the caps for lodging and meals. Decide within 3 days, on the claim's screen or with the approval card in your chat. When you reject, tell the claimant the reason yourself.
